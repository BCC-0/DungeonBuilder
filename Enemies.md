# Level 1) Farm theme:

- Heads:

| Name | Behavior | Difficulty | Adjustable properties |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cow | Lets out a moo occasionally, weakening the player’s damage. | Simple | Timer length, weakning percentage. |
| Chicken | Shoots an egg at the player. | Simple | Eyesight length, attacking speed. |
| Pig | Squeals when attacked, this alerts all enemies withing range. Doesn’t attack, so only hurts enemy when it tries to walk through. | Simple | Alertion reach. |
| Dog | Buffs enemies within reach with extra damage. | Simple | Buff reach. |
| Goat | If player is within eyesight it will trigger an immediate attack. The goat will move towards the player with their body’s move type. | Simple | Eyesight length, attacking speed. |
- Bodies:

| Name | Behavior | Difficulty | Adjustable properties |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cow | Simple movement with speed. Player can’t simply walk through (is like a wall). | Simple | Speed. |
| Chicken | Simple movement with speed. Leaves behind eggs that deal damage when walking through or after a set time. | Simple | Speed, egg damage, crack time. |
| Pig | Simple movement with speed. When hit, the player takes no damage, but gives knockback if it was melee!
 | Simple | Speed, knockback. |
| Dog | Walks towards other enemies with speed. | Simple | Speed. |
| Goat | When player tries to walk through, the player takes damage and knockback. | Simple | Speed, damage, knockback. |
